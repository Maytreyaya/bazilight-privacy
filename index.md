# BaziLight — Privacy Policy

_Effective: 2026-11-15 (replaces the text of 2026-09-10)_

BaziLight ("the app") is a personal companion built around Bazi (八字), the
Book of Changes (易經) and a daily practice of attention. This policy says what
the app does with information on your device. In short: **your records stay on
your phone. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no
server of its own, and it does not go online.**

## 1. What the app keeps — on your device only

| What | Why | Where |
|------|-----|-------|
| For each saved chart: date, time and place of birth (city, coordinates), and, if you give them, a name and a gender | To work out the chart, the day's reading and birthday reminders | The app's own storage on your device |
| Daily check-ins (how the day felt, a word, an optional note, the date) | To show you your own pattern over time | The app's own storage on your device |
| Readings of the Book of Changes (the sign, the lines, an optional question and note) | Your own history of readings | The app's own storage on your device |
| Practice marks, rituals of the solar terms, answers in the Reading | To show what you have done | The app's own storage on your device |
| Settings (language, text size, notification time and switches, where you stopped reading) | To remember your choices | The app's own storage on your device |

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it.

## 2. What the app does not collect

- No account, e-mail address or sign-in.
- No analytics, crash reports or advertising identifiers.
- No location. The birth place you choose is used to work out solar time for
  the chart; the app never reads where your device is.
- No contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
- The app makes no network requests. The Android version does not even ask
  for permission to use the internet.

## 3. Copies you make yourself

In **Settings → My charts** you can save a copy of your records as a file
("Save a copy") or a table of your charts ("Charts as a table"). The app
hands the file to your phone's own "share" sheet, and it goes only where you
send it — to your files, a drive, a message. "Restore a copy" reads a file
you pick and adds its records to the app. The app reads no other file.

A copy holds birth data, including the birth data of other people whose
charts you saved. Keep it as you would keep any personal document.

## 4. Backups made by your phone

Android can include an app's data in the backup of your device to your own
Google account, if you have turned such backups on in your phone's settings.
That backup is made by Android, is kept in your account and is governed by
Google's terms; the app neither starts it nor can read it. You can turn it
off in your phone's settings.

## 5. Motion sensor

On the screens where you toss the coins by shaking the phone, and where the
picture of the chart tilts slightly with the phone, the app reads the motion
sensor. The readings are used at that moment to move the picture and are not
kept or sent anywhere.

## 6. Notifications

If you turn them on, the reading of the day, birthday reminders and the
starts of the solar terms are scheduled **on your device** by its operating
system. No push service and no server are involved. You can turn them off in
**Settings → Notifications** or in your phone's settings.

## 7. Permissions the app asks for

| Permission | What for |
|------------|----------|
| Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`) | To show the reminders you turned on |
| Exact alarms (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`, Android 12+) | To bring the reading of the day at the time you chose; without it a reminder may come a few minutes later |
| Start after restart (`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`) | To set your reminders again after the phone restarts |
| Vibration (`VIBRATE`) | So that a reminder can vibrate, as your phone's own settings allow |

## 8. Deleting your data

You can remove a chart in **Settings → My charts**. Removing the app removes
everything it kept on the device. A backup made by your phone (section 4) and
copies you saved yourself (section 3) are yours to delete where you keep them.

## 9. Children

The app is not directed at children and knowingly collects nothing from
anyone: it has no accounts and nothing leaves the device.

## 10. Texts and libraries

The words of the Book of Changes follow the classical text as Zhu Xi read it;
the Dao De Jing follows the Wang Bi edition; the English and Ukrainian
renderings were made for this app. The app is built on open-source
libraries, whose licences are listed in **About → Licences**. None of them
receives your data.

## 11. Changes

If a later version adds anything that works online — purchases through the
store, an optional sign-in, music played from another service — this policy
will be changed before that version is released, and the new text will be
linked from the **About** screen.
